«Мои 15 вершин Алатау» is a programme from the Kaz Alpine Club, run with the Outdoor
Center Almaty, that takes fifteen well known summits of the Zailiysky Alatau and sorts
them into three tiers. You climb them, you post the photo with the hashtag, you collect
a badge per summit.

It works because it turns a vague ambition into a finite list. As of mid 2024 around
2,500 people had joined in and 57 had finished all fifteen.

I wanted the list in one place as something I could actually tick off, so here it is.

## Table of contents

## Bronze

Accessible summits, the entry point. All five sit between 3000 and 3700 metres and are
day objectives from the city.

- [ ] **Bukreev Peak** — Пик Букреева — 3010 m
- [ ] **Furmanov Peak** — Пик Фурманова — 3053 m
- [ ] **Kumbel** — Пик Кумбель — 3200 m
- [ ] **Chimbulachka** — Чимбулачка — ~3450 m
- [ ] **Bolshoi Almatinsky Peak (BAP)** — Большой Алматинский пик — ~3680 m

## Silver

Real endurance days. The floor jumps to 3871 m and the tier tops out above 4300 m, so
this is where altitude starts doing the deciding.

- [ ] **Titov Peak** — Пик Титова — 3871 m
- [ ] **Turist Peak** — Пик Турист — 3954 m
- [ ] **Karnizny Peak** — Пик Карнизный — 4075 m
- [ ] **Heroes of Panfilov Peak** — Пик Героев Панфиловцев — 4120 m
- [ ] **Sovetov Peak** — Пик Советов — 4317 m

## Gold

For experienced mountain travellers.

- [ ] **Kamensky Peak** — Пик Каменский — 3674 m
- [ ] **Kargalinsky Peak** — Пик Каргалинский — 3675 m
- [ ] **Khrishchaty Peak** — Пик Хрищатого — 3950 m
- [ ] **Molodezhny Peak** — Пик Молодежный — 4147 m
- [ ] **Nursultan Peak** — Пик Нурсултан — 4376 m

## Gold is not simply higher

Read the three lists as numbers and something jumps out. Kamensky at 3674 m and
Kargalinsky at 3675 m are both **lower than every single silver peak**, and lower than
four of them by several hundred metres.

So the tiers are not sorted by altitude. Silver is graded on endurance, the long day and
the thin air. Gold is graded on the nature of the ground: terrain, route finding and the
skills the summit demands of you. A 3674 m gold peak can ask more of you than a 4317 m
silver one.

That matters for planning. Finishing silver does not qualify you for gold, because the
thing gold tests is not the thing silver trained.

## Working through it

The ordering inside each tier is close to an acclimatisation ladder if you follow it. The
bronze tier climbs gently from 3010 m to roughly 3680 m, which is a sensible progression
before the silver peaks put you above 4000 m.

Two honest notes on the numbers above. Elevations differ between sources, and Titov Peak
in particular is quoted as both 3252 m and 3871 m depending on where you look. I have used
the figure the programme itself publishes. Chimbulachka and BAP are widely cited as
approximate, hence the tildes.

And the obvious thing that a checklist cannot carry: this is a list, not route information.
Nothing here says anything about conditions, approach, season or what any individual summit
actually requires on the day. Get that from people who have been on the route recently.

## Print it

The three lists above are the whole programme. Fifteen boxes, five per tier, roughly a
season's worth of weekends if the weather cooperates and rather more if it does not.
